📌 About The Project

AI Interviewer is an intelligent interview preparation application that helps students, job seekers, and professionals practice for real-world interviews.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence to simulate interview experiences, generate relevant questions, and provide feedback to help users identify their strengths and areas for improvement.

The main goal is to make interview preparation more accessible, interactive, and effective.

✨ Features
🤖 AI-Powered Interviews: Experience interactive mock interviews with AI.
💬 Smart Question Generation: Get interview questions based on selected roles and topics.
📝 Personalized Feedback: Receive suggestions to improve your answers.
🎯 Role-Based Preparation: Practice for different job roles and industries.
📊 Performance Analysis: Review your interview performance and identify improvement areas.
⚡ Interactive Experience: Enjoy a clean and user-friendly interface.
📱 Responsive Design: Access the application across different devices.
